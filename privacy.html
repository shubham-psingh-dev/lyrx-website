Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

1. Introduction

LYRx ("LYRx", "we", "our", or "the application") is a desktop music application currently under active development.

This Privacy Policy explains the information that LYRx may access and use when you use account features such as email/password authentication or Google Sign-In.

2. Information You Provide

When you create or use a LYRx account, you may provide information such as your email address, password and display name.

Authentication credentials are processed through Firebase Authentication.

3. Google Sign-In Data

If you choose Sign in with Google, LYRx requests basic identity information required for authentication.

  • Google account identifier
  • Name or display name
  • Email address
  • Google profile image URL

LYRx does not request access to Gmail messages, Google Drive files, Google Calendar, contacts or unrelated Google account content through the current Google Sign-In implementation.

4. How Google User Data Is Used

Google account information obtained through Google Sign-In is used to authenticate you, identify your LYRx account, display account information and maintain the signed-in experience.

If a Google profile image is available, LYRx may save a local copy for display inside the desktop application.

5. Firebase Authentication

LYRx uses Firebase Authentication to provide account authentication functionality.

6. Local Application Data

Some application preferences and profile-related information may be stored locally on the computer where LYRx is installed.

7. Data Sharing

LYRx does not sell Google user data.

Information may be processed by authentication providers necessary to provide account functionality, including Google and Firebase.

LYRx does not use Google Sign-In information for advertising.

8. Data Security

LYRx uses established authentication services instead of building its own online password-verification system.

9. Your Choices

Google Sign-In is optional. You may sign out of LYRx using the account interface.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as LYRx features and data practices change.

11. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy may be sent to the developer contact information associated with LYRx.
