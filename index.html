ABOUT LYRx

Built for people who live through music.

LYRx is an independently developed desktop music application focused on creating a modern, enjoyable and organized listening experience.

The application combines music discovery, library management, favorites, playlists, account authentication and additional music-focused features in a single interface.

LYRx is currently under active development.
